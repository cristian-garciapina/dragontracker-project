The Vanguard Manual

How the alliance operates.

Everything you need to use the Eternal Vanguard tools: the site at eternal-vanguard.com, the Discord bot, and the grading system that keeps the alliance accountable. Written for members, not engineers.

The five grades Platinum Gold Silver Bronze Matte
Article I

The platform.

Eternal Vanguard has its own website and its own Discord bot. They talk to each other so everything stays in sync.

The website is the record. It knows who is in the alliance, what everyone has done this season, and where each person ranks. Staff use it to review new arrivals and to load the weekly game data.

The Discord bot is where the roster comes to life inside the chat. It gives you the right role automatically, translates messages between languages, tracks who's coming to events, and lets you look up any player without leaving Discord.

Once you connect your Discord account to your website account, the two stay linked forever. Your grade role updates on its own. Your Discord nickname matches your in-game name. You don't have to do anything.

Article II

Getting started.

Three steps between joining and being fully set up. Plan for a few hours between step two and step three while staff review your account.

  1. Create your website account. Go to eternal-vanguard.com/signup. You will need three things: your in-game name, your character ID, and a screenshot.

    Your character ID is a number Call of Dragons shows next to your name on your Profile screen — it's how the game and the alliance identify you. Copy it exactly.

    The screenshot is your in-game Profile screen. You reach it by tapping your own portrait in the top-left of the game. Only you can see this exact screen — that's how staff verify the account is really yours.
  2. Wait for approval. Staff receive a notification the moment you submit. They check your screenshot against your details and approve, promote, or reject the request. You'll get an email once it's done. Usually takes under a day.
  3. Link your Discord. Once approved, log in, open eternal-vanguard.com/profile, and click Link Discord. The bot notices within seconds and gives you the @Verified role plus the grade role that matches your standing. From then on, your Discord nickname automatically mirrors your in-game name.
Note
If you are not yet in the alliance and want to join, use eternal-vanguard.com/apply instead. That form is open to anyone — no account needed.
Article III

Your grade.

Every active member has a grade, updated with each new load of game data. It's based on one number, and one number only.

The formula

Your M/P% is your season merits divided by your starting power, times one hundred. That's it. No hidden weights, no engagement index, no seven-way scoring — one ratio, one grade.

Worked example
You started the season with 100M power and have earned 8M merits so far.
Your M/P% is 8 ÷ 100 × 100 = 8% — that's Gold ({{ threshold_a }}% or higher).
Why
Merits on their own would always favour the biggest accounts. Merits divided by power rewards effort relative to what you brought. A 50M player who fights hard can grade higher than a 200M player who coasts.

  • On Discord: your coloured role next to your name, or type /me to get the full breakdown.
Article IV

The website, page by page.

Once you're logged in, five pages matter.

eternal-vanguard.com/dashboard
The season at a glance. How many players are at each grade, who's at the top, and what the alliance average looks like. This is the first page staff open every morning.
eternal-vanguard.com/roster
The full member list. Filter by grade, search by name or character ID, tap any ID to copy it. On mobile, the name column stays visible when you scroll sideways.
eternal-vanguard.com/profile
Your own account. Change your password, connect or disconnect Discord, see your current grade.
eternal-vanguard.com/player/<id>
Any player's history. Their grade over past seasons, their event participation, their power growth. You reach this by clicking any name in the roster.
eternal-vanguard.com/farms
The farm accounts. Every account at or below {{ farm_threshold_m }}M starting power. They're not part of the grading, but they're listed here for reference.
Article V

Your profile, in detail.

The eternal-vanguard.com/profile page is where you control the link between your website account and everything else on the platform.

Connecting Discord

Click Link Discord, allow the app when Discord asks, and you're done. The bot notices within seconds and updates your roles and nickname.

Disconnecting Discord

You can unlink whenever you want. The @Verified role and your grade role are removed immediately. Your website account stays intact — nothing else changes.

Forgot your password?

Use the Forgot password link on the login page. A reset email arrives within a minute — check your spam folder if it doesn't show up.

Article VI

Discord commands.

A slash command is something you type in Discord starting with a /. Discord shows a menu of what's available as you type. All commands here reply only to you by default — nobody else sees the answer unless you tap the "Post publicly" button.

Article VII

Speaking across languages.

K193 is a mixed-language kingdom. The bot translates any message on demand, without cluttering the conversation.

  1. Add a flag as a reaction. On any message, react with a flag emoji — 🇬🇧, 🇫🇷, 🇪🇸, 🇻🇳, and about twenty others. The bot reads the flag as "please translate this into that language."
  2. The translation shows up in the right channel. Public messages get translated in #traductions. Staff-only messages get translated in #staff-traductions. A staff message can never leak into a public channel by accident.
  3. Jump back to the original. Each translation has a Voir le message original ↗ button that takes you straight to the message it came from, in case you need the context.
Tip
If two people ask for the same translation, each of you gets your own copy tagged with your name. Nobody misses it in the scrollback.
Article VIII

Events and responses.

When staff schedule an event — a ranking, a behemoth run, a gate, a spire — the bot posts it in #events with two buttons.

  • I'm in — you're counted as coming. Your name shows up in the "Coming" list on the event's post.
  • Can't make it — you're counted as out. You appear in the "Not coming" list.

Change your mind whenever you want, right up until the event's over. The morning after the event ends (UTC), the buttons freeze and the post is marked closed.

Reminders

You'll get automatic reminders in the channel: one the morning before the event, one an hour before if there's a specific start time, and one on the morning of the last day.

Verify
The buttons only work if you have the @Verified role. If you haven't linked your Discord to the website yet, the bot will tell you to visit eternal-vanguard.com/profile first.
Article IX

Staff duties.

R4 and R5 have a few extra tools. Almost everything can be done from either the website or Discord, whichever is faster in the moment.

Reviewing new arrivals

Two channels feed the review queue:

  • Applications come from eternal-vanguard.com/apply — people asking to join the alliance.
  • Signups come from eternal-vanguard.com/signup — existing alliance members asking for a website account.

Both send a notification to #staff-alerts, with the submitted screenshot attached. Click Accept or Reject with reason right on Discord, or open eternal-vanguard.com/staff/applications and eternal-vanguard.com/staff/registrations on the website for the full view.

First
Whichever action fires first — website or Discord — closes the record. Don't double-click.

Loading the weekly game data

The bot does this on its own. Every night, it pulls the latest export from the Farlight portal, checks it for duplicates, loads it into the database, and posts a summary in #audit-log. If everything went well, the message says so. If something went wrong — a corrupted file, an expired token, a conflict with a manual upload — the message says that too, and pings staff.

You do not need to do anything for the normal weekly cycle. Just glance at #audit-log occasionally to confirm the bot is happy.

If you need to force a pull right now

Run /farlight-pull-force on Discord (staff only). The bot fetches the current data immediately, ignoring the schedule, and posts the same summary as usual to #audit-log.

If you need to upload a file by hand

Occasionally you may want to load a specific export that the automatic pull didn't catch. Open eternal-vanguard.com/staff/upload, drag the file in, and confirm the replacement if the site asks — that happens when a file for the same period already exists.

The Farlight token expires from time to time

The bot uses a personal token to authenticate to the Farlight portal. Every couple of months it expires. The bot warns you in #staff-alerts a week before that happens, so you have time to log in to Farlight, grab a fresh token, and update it via /staff/settings. Instructions come with the warning.

Running events

Open eternal-vanguard.com/staff/events, pick the current season, and click New event. Choose the kind (ranking, behemoth, gate, spire), the dates, and an optional start time in UTC. Save it — the bot posts the response embed in #events within seconds.

To cancel, click the Delete button on the event's page or the ✕ on the events list. The Discord post disappears automatically.

Season lifecycle

Starting a new season takes four steps:

  1. Upload a one-day snapshot of the season's first day (start date = end date).
  2. Close the current season on eternal-vanguard.com/staff/seasons.
  3. Create the new season, and point Start snapshot at the file you just uploaded.
  4. Activate it. The nightly pulls from that point on will fill in the new scoreboard.
Article X

Common questions.

How often are grades updated?

Every night. The bot loads the latest game data automatically around 03:00 UTC, and grades are recalculated right after. If you log in in the morning, you're looking at yesterday's activity.

Why is my grade different from what I expected?

Grades update with each new load of data. If you built a lot of power right before the season started, your denominator is high — your M/P% will lag behind your merit total. This is intentional and won't change.

My in-game name just changed. Do I need to update the website?

No. The next data load reads your current name from the export and updates it everywhere, including your Discord nickname.

I disconnected Discord by mistake. What now?

Go back to eternal-vanguard.com/profile and link it again. Your grade role comes back on its own.

The bot isn't responding to my slash command.

Discord sometimes takes a few minutes to sync new commands after the bot is updated. Try /ping first — if that works, wait a moment and try your command again.

Can I hide my grade from other members?

No. Grades are public by design. The alliance operates on shared accountability — everyone sees everyone's standing, including staff. That's the point.

Where does my data go?

Everything is stored on a single server run by the alliance owner. No third-party analytics, no ads, no data resale. Only your game stats (from the Farlight export) and what you submit at signup.

Article XI

Getting help.

If something's broken, or you're stuck, ask. The alliance is small enough that a direct message gets a real answer, usually within the hour.

  • Send a direct message to any R4 or R5 on Discord.
  • Post in #general if it's not urgent and might help others.
  • For account issues you'd rather keep private, email contact@eternal-vanguard.com.
Soon
A dedicated support ticket system is on the roadmap — private channels between you and staff, tracked from open to close. Until it lands, DMs are the way.
