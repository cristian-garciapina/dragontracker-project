The Handbook

How the alliance operates.

Everything you need to use the {{ alliance.name }} website: the dashboard, the roster, and the grading system that keeps the alliance accountable. Written for members, not engineers.

The five grades Platinum Gold Silver Bronze Matte
Article I

The platform.

This is the {{ alliance.name }} website. It is where the alliance tracks scores, seasons, applications, and members.

The website keeps the records. It knows who is in the alliance, what everyone has done this season, and where each player ranks. Staff use it to review new arrivals and to load the weekly game data.

Once you link your Discord account to your website account, the two stay connected. Your grade role updates on its own. Your Discord nickname matches your in-game name. You do not have to do anything.

Article II

Getting started.

There are three steps between joining and being fully set up. Expect a few hours between step 2 and step 3 while staff review your request.

  1. Create your website account. Go to //signup. You will need three things: your in-game name, your character ID, and a screenshot.

    Your character ID is the number Call of Dragons shows next to your name on your Profile screen — the game uses it to identify you. Copy it exactly, without spaces.

    The screenshot is your in-game Profile screen — the one you open by tapping your own portrait in the top-left of the game. Only you can see that exact screen from your account, which is how staff confirm the account is really yours.
  2. Wait for approval. Staff receive a notification the moment you submit. They compare your screenshot against your details and either approve, promote, or reject the request. You will get an email as soon as it is done — usually within a few hours.
  3. Link your Discord. Once approved, log in, open //profile, and click Link Discord. The bot notices within seconds and gives you the @Verified role plus the grade role that matches your current standing. From then on, your Discord nickname automatically mirrors your in-game name.
Note
If you are not yet in the alliance and want to join, use //apply instead. That form is open to anyone — no account needed.
Article III

Your grade.

Every active member has a grade, updated each time new game data is loaded. It is based on one number, and one number only.

The formula

Your M/P% is your season merits divided by your starting power, times one hundred. That is the whole system. No hidden weights, no engagement index, no seven-way scoring — one ratio, one grade.

Worked example
You started the season with 100M power and have earned 8M merits so far.
Your M/P% is 8 ÷ 100 × 100 = 8% — that is Gold ({{ threshold_a }}% or higher).
Why
Merits alone would always favour the biggest accounts. Merits divided by power rewards effort relative to what you brought. A 50M player who fights hard can grade higher than a 200M player who coasts.

Where to see yours

  • On the website: //profile for your own grade, //roster for everyone's.
  • On Discord: the coloured role next to your name, or type /me to get the full breakdown.
Article IV

The website, page by page.

Once you are logged in, five pages matter.

//dashboard
The season at a glance. How many players sit at each grade, who is at the top, and what the alliance average looks like. This is the first page staff open every morning.
//roster
The full member list. Filter by grade, search by name or character ID, tap any ID to copy it. On mobile, the name column stays visible when you scroll sideways.
//profile
Your own account. Change your password, connect or disconnect Discord, see your current grade.
//player/<id>
Any player's history. Their grade over past seasons, their event participation, their power growth. You reach this by clicking any name in the roster.
//farms
The farm accounts. Every account at or below {{ farm_threshold_m }}M starting power. They are not part of the grading, but they are listed here for reference.
Article V

Your profile, in detail.

The //profile page is where you control the link between your website account and everything else on the platform.

Connecting Discord

Click Link Discord, allow the app when Discord asks, and that's it. The bot notices within seconds and updates your roles and nickname.

Disconnecting Discord

You can unlink whenever you want. The @Verified role and your grade role are removed immediately. Your website account stays intact — nothing else changes.

Forgot your password?

Use the Forgot password link on the login page. A reset email arrives within a minute — check your spam folder if it does not show up.

Article IX

Staff duties.

R4 and R5 have a few extra tools. Almost everything can be done from either the website or Discord, whichever is faster in the moment.

Reviewing new arrivals

Two channels feed the review queue:

  • Applications come from //apply — people asking to join the alliance.
  • Signups come from //signup — existing alliance members asking for a website account.

Both send a notification to #staff-alerts, with the submitted screenshot attached. Click Accept or Reject with reason right there on Discord, or open //staff/applications and //staff/registrations on the website for the full view.

First
Whichever action fires first — website or Discord — closes the record. Do not double-click.

Loading the weekly game data

The bot handles this on its own. Every night, it pulls the latest export from the Farlight portal, checks it for duplicates, loads it into the database, and posts a summary in #audit-log. If everything went well, the message says so. If something went wrong — a corrupted file, an expired token, a conflict with a manual upload — the message says that too, and pings staff.

You do not need to do anything for the normal weekly cycle. Just glance at #audit-log from time to time to confirm the bot is happy.

If you need to force a pull right now

Run /farlight-pull-force on Discord (staff only). The bot fetches the current data immediately, ignoring the schedule, and posts the same kind of summary to #audit-log.

If you need to upload a file by hand

Occasionally you may want to load a specific export that the automatic pull did not catch. Open //staff/upload, drop the file in, and confirm the replacement if the site asks — that happens when a file for the same period already exists.

The Farlight token expires from time to time

The bot uses a personal token to authenticate against the Farlight portal. Every couple of months, that token expires. The bot warns you in #staff-alerts a week ahead, so you have time to log in to Farlight, grab a fresh token, and update it via /staff/settings. Instructions come with the warning.

Running events

Open //staff/events, pick the current season, and click New event. Choose the kind (ranking, behemoth, gate, spire), the dates, and an optional start time in UTC. Save it — the bot posts the response embed in #events within seconds.

To cancel, click the Delete button on the event's page or the ✕ on the events list. The Discord post disappears automatically.

Season lifecycle

Starting a new season takes four steps:

  1. Upload a one-day snapshot of the season's first day (start date = end date).
  2. Close the current season on //staff/seasons.
  3. Create the new season, and point Start snapshot at the file you just uploaded.
  4. Activate it. The nightly pulls from that point on will fill in the new scoreboard.
Article X

Common questions.

How often are grades updated?

Every night. The bot loads the latest game data automatically around 03:00 UTC, and grades are recalculated right after. If you check the site in the morning, you are looking at yesterday's activity.

Why is my grade different from what I expected?

Grades update with each new load of data. If you built a lot of power right before the season started, your denominator is high — your M/P% will lag behind your merit total. This is intentional and will not change.

My in-game name just changed. Do I need to update the website?

No. The next data load reads your current name from the export and updates it everywhere, including your Discord nickname.

I disconnected Discord by mistake. What now?

Go back to //profile and link it again. Your grade role comes back on its own.

The bot is not responding to my slash command.

Discord sometimes takes a few minutes to sync new commands after the bot has been updated. Try /ping first — if that works, wait a moment and try your command again.

Can I hide my grade from other members?

No. Grades are public by design. The alliance operates on shared accountability — everyone sees everyone's standing, including staff. That is the point.

Where does my data go?

Everything is stored on a single server run by the alliance owner. No third-party analytics, no ads, no data resale. Only your game stats (from the Farlight export) and what you submit at signup.

Article XI

Getting help.

If something is broken, or you are stuck, ask. The alliance is small enough that a direct message gets a real answer, usually within the hour.

  • Send a direct message to any R4 or R5 on Discord.
  • Post in #general if it is not urgent and might help others.
  • For account issues you would rather keep private, email contact@/.
